🪙 PAYDAY!
Bangkok Budget Challenge · Fictional characters · All amounts in Thai baht

Teacher guide

2 minutes to start. Open the game, pick a character and read January’s payday screen. Students can play alone or share one device. Their goal is to get through 12 months and finish with cash, savings and a good life score.

About 86 minutes of play. Aim for about seven minutes per month. Each month has six decisions, a payday and a choice about savings. Put June on the board when the class reaches it so they can compare borrowers with renters. In September a national flood cuts wages or drains savings, depending on the character. Teams that finish early can replay a different character or compare their biggest surprise bills using the score table.

2 minutes to compare. Ask two teams to call out annual score, savings, stress and how many months they spent in overdraft. Ask whether the September flood cost them wages or savings, and how they covered the shortfall.

The final score gives up to 40 for savings, 40 for life points and 20 for a cash buffer after December’s unpaid commitments. Each month closed in overdraft costs two points. A final shortfall costs another ten. The year is fictional. For the simulation the central bank’s one percentage point increase passes through in full to the variable mortgages. Actual bank rates and repayment terms differ. Car loans use fixed payments and a simple reducing-balance model. Bank of Thailand explanation of floating-rate debt.

Salary and budget sources

We use rounded fictional take-home pay informed by employer postings. Listed pay and take-home pay can differ. Each cost is a scenario assumption. For example, weekly food of ฿1,150–฿1,550 is about ฿55–฿74 per meal for three meals a day, including home meals. The hotel receptionist, motorbike mechanic and graphic designer use fictional take-home pay examples, with Bangkok graphic designer listings as an additional reference. Travel combines trips by bus, train or on foot. Fixed bills are charged once, and the four weekly costs cover meals, travel and household basics.
